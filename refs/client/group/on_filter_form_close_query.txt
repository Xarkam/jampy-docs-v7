==========================
on_filter_form_close_query
==========================

.. js:function:: Group.on_filter_form_close_query(item)

    :domain: client
    :language: javascript
    :class: :js:class:`Group`

Description
===========

The ``on_filter_form_close_query`` event is triggered by the
:js!meth:`close_filter_form <Item.close_filter_form>`
method of the item.

Parameter
=========

:guilabel:`item` is the item that triggered the event.
