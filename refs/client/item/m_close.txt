=====
close
=====

.. js:method:: Item.close()

    :domain: client
    :language: javascript
    :class: :js:class:`Item`

Description
===========

Call ``сlose`` to close an item dataset. After dataset is closed the
:js:attr:`active <Item.active>`
property is ``false``.
